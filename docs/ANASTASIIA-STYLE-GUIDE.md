# ANASTASIIA STYLE GUIDE
Derived from direct inspection of the eight primary sketchbook references in `/source/anastasiia-reference/`. These pages are the visual authority for every ink, wash, and annotation decision on the site. Campaign composites are analysed separately at the end.

## Page-by-page observations

### IMG_5629 — Tangalle market spread (mixed-subject authority)
- Left page: watercolor-first sacks of produce (green chillies, peas) with almost no outline; right page: dense pen-hatched eggplant pile and bitter melon in pure ink, colour withheld.
- Portrait of the tobacco seller: face carries the most finished paint on the spread; shirt stripes dissolve into loose broken line below the chest — detail budget spent on the face only.
- Hand-lettered "TANGALLE" in brush caps; cursive caption "A man selling dried tobacco leaves at the market in"; Latin botanical labels in pencil: "Thai green eggplant (Solanum macrocarpon)", "Bitter melon (Momordica charantia)".
- Painted price signs ("250/60", "250|130/-") treated as objects, red hand-drawn numerals.
- Cattle at right edge cropped by the page — subjects are allowed to run off.
- Lesson: one screen may hold portrait + object + botany + text at different finish levels without becoming one illustration.

### IMG_5630 — Kyoto spread (ink-density authority)
- Pagoda vignette: heaviest ink on the spread — near-black silhouette with warm lantern yellows punched through; sits in a soft sunset wash bubble that ends irregularly, far inside the page edge.
- Sushi plate floats in negative space; the tray's blue-grey shadow wash crosses the centre fold without concern.
- Large calligraphic 京都 characters function as composition, not caption; small cursive "Exploring Kyoto" beside them.
- Curved hand-drawn arrows connect notes to subjects ("Sushi at the Tsukiji Fish Market" ↗, "Hazaridaru in Takayama" ↘).
- Chopsticks drawn at full length diagonally across both pages — a single object is allowed to organise the whole spread.
- Lesson: architecture is drawn with emotional dark masses, never CAD-clean contour.

### IMG_5631 — Madiha Beach panorama (PRIMARY landscape/water authority)
- Sky: one pale graded blue wash, granulating, with large untouched paper clouds; no line at all in the sky.
- Water: horizontal ribbons of two blues + untouched paper sparkle; contour only where water meets sand.
- Palms: dark green calligraphic strokes over lighter wash, trunks in broken pen line; foliage masses stay loose.
- Foreground structures: 3–4 line weights, colour applied past the line edges; whole left foreground fades to bare pencil construction at the bottom corner.
- Hand-lettered "MADIHA BEACH" title on a painted label strip; pencil note "Surf boards at Madihy Beach" with arrow.
- **Critical rule for video→sketch: broad washes + selective contour + untouched areas. Never full-frame edge detection.**

### IMG_5632 — Central Market of Kandy (portrait/human authority)
- Five human studies at five finish levels on one spread: environmental vignette (green wash dominant), two painted portraits (headscarf woman, smiling young man — full watercolor, observed light), one head study (ink + partial wash, red collar accent), one seated figure in pure crosshatched ink with no colour.
- Backgrounds: none. Figures float on paper; at most a coloured shadow.
- "CENTRAL MARKET" hand-lettered caps + cursive "of Kandy" beneath.
- Lesson: people are documentary and specific, never glossy; the host section should use this register.

### IMG_5633 — Takayama spread (architecture-in-landscape authority)
- Stone maneki-neko sculpture study at left: warm grey washes, pencil construction still visible through paint; sits in a green wash bubble.
- Centre: farmhouse village vignette — thatched roofs in grey-green wash, structure lines only on roof ridges and fence; carp streamers add one saturated accent.
- Right: temple gate roof drawn in fine pen with wash held to underside shadows; drawing simply stops mid-structure at the page edge.
- Purple Hama-rikyu Gardens entry stamp kept as collage element; pencil "Takayama" floats top-centre.
- Lesson for Thiksey: architectural hierarchy = dark roof masses + selective structural line + vegetation strokes around; partial drawings are complete.

### IMG_5634 — Polwatta / Gal Oya spread (scale-shift authority)
- Detailed red motorbike (object, macro) beside a wide lake panorama (landscape) beside a painted fisherman portrait with fish (person) — three scales, no dividers, one spread.
- Rain rendered as vertical dry-brush streaks of pale blue behind the bike — weather as texture.
- Lake: mirror-band of green-blue wash, hills in one flat blue-grey silhouette; enormous bare-paper sky.
- Long cursive note: "Made a sketch of this old motocycle while waiting for the heavy rain to stop." Hand-lettered "POLWATTA" boxed; "Boat safari at GAL OYA VALLEY NATIONAL PARK" in mixed cursive/caps with sub-note "The most untouched of all Sri-Lanka's national parks."
- Lesson: the site may cut OBJECT → LANDSCAPE → PERSON without section boundaries.

### IMG_5635 — Tangalle fishing boats (PRIMARY line/structure authority)
- Hull perspective built from confident curved contour lines; rigging = single unbroken fine pen strokes crossing the sky with no wash behind them.
- Saturated hull stripes (yellow/red/blue/teal) painted wet, edges bleeding slightly past the ink.
- Boat name "KIVEN POTHA" lettered within the drawing — text as observed object.
- Left page: standing figure beside beached hull; ground = two tone bands + broken shadow; background sea = one teal band; large areas of the port left as white paper.
- Water shadow under bow: loose dark blue blobs, not reflection rendering.
- Lesson for animated SVGs: separate construction pass (light, wobbly, open-ended) from primary pass (dark, decisive); rigging-style single strokes read beautifully when drawn over time.

### IMG_5636 — Village life spread (PRIMARY botany/field-note authority)
- Banana botanical study: leaf canopy from top edge, fruit bunch fully modelled in greens, root/stem in pencil-only — one specimen shown at three finish levels; isolated on white.
- Rubber-stamped dates "28 FEB. 2024", "25 FEB. 2024", "27 FEB. 2024" — date stamps as page furniture.
- Map-pin doodles + pencil notes: "Roosters running at 📍Mouang Ngoy village", "📍Thakhek's Everyday life scenes"; long S-curved arrows.
- Roosters: tail feathers in saturated teal/indigo, heads red-orange; small grass tufts ground them, otherwise floating.
- Cyclist with loaded cart: mid-density study, blue shadow wash pooled under the wheels.
- ~40% of the spread is untouched paper.
- Lesson: botanical content is observed and specific (specimen isolation, Latin-name register, date stamps), never decorative garnish.

## Extracted grammar (binding rules)

**INK** — confident, irregular, variable-weight contour; visible pencil construction beneath; hatching reserved for darkest masses; lines frequently left open; detail budget concentrated on one focal subject per composition.

**WATERCOLOR** — transparent; paper shows through everywhere; edges irregular and granulating; colour routinely escapes the line; some subjects colour-only (no line), some line-only (no colour); accent saturation limited to small areas (hulls, roosters, lanterns); no digital gradients, no global filters.

**COMPOSITION** — asymmetric; multiple scales coexist; 30–50% negative space typical; page edges and centre fold visible and used; subjects cropped by edges; wash "bubbles" end far inside the frame; no grid.

**ANNOTATION** — three text registers: (1) hand-lettered brush caps for place names, (2) cursive pencil/pen for observations, (3) pencil Latin labels for botany; plus curved arrows, date stamps, map-pin doodles, price-sign objects. Notes appear near, and after, the thing observed.

**TYPICAL SPREAD FORMULA** — landscape + object + person + botany/animal + text, at deliberately different finish levels.

## Palette observed in the references
Warm ivory paper; grey-green and slate-blue water ribbons; sap/olive foliage greens; ochre-sand grounds; one hot accent per spread (marigold orange, hull red, lantern yellow); ink is warm near-black, never pure #000. This maps cleanly onto the mandated Ladakh palette (§13 of the master spec).
