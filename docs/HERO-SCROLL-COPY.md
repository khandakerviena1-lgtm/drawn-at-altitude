## Two things to settle before this ships

**The film closes on Nubra Valley, and Nubra is not on the itinerary.** The nine
days are Arrival, Thiksey, Leh, Hemis, Alchi, Igoo, Pangong, Apricot, Tea. So
the final beat — the one that promises what the guest leaves with — plays over a
place the retreat does not visit, two beats after a line that names Pangong.
**Recommendation: swap the closing spread** for one on the itinerary. A hook
this precise cannot afford that question.

**Nothing above invents a fact.** Sea buckthorn is sour (the artist's own note
reads *tart and bright*), apricot and tea are itinerary days, Thiksey's caption
is quoted from the page itself. Where a detail could not be verified — how long
the bluethroat stayed, the hour of the light — the line was written to work
without it rather than filled in.
