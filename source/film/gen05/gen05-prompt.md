## What this generation is, and what it is not

The verbatim GEN 05 is VEGETATION → FIRE → PIGMENT, beginning from an ochre
plant and ending on *"a loaded brush leaving the palette toward ivory paper"*.
**That version is no longer reachable.** The sketchbook chain ended Gen 04 on a
drawn page, not on vegetation, and frame-locking means the start is fixed.

What is reachable, and what this file generates: **the painted pink of the page
becoming the real red pigment it was made from.** One material transformation,
comfortably inside the brief's one-or-two budget — the discipline whose absence
produced Gen 03's sea horizon.

## Three things to judge, and one to decide afterwards

1. **The destination is wool dye, not watercolour.** This is madder root
   prepared for dyeing fleece, filmed at We Are Kal — real Ladakhi pigment
   ground from the earth, but not paint on an artist's palette. The reading it
   supports is a good one: *the colour in the sketchbook and the colour in the
   textile come from the same ground.* It is not the reading the brief wrote.
   **Flag at review** — if it is wrong, the fix is real footage of Anastasiia's
   palette, which does not exist yet and belongs on the asset request list.
2. **Colour swing.** The page is pale blue-green; the destination is saturated
   red. This is why the push targets the **pink tamarix**, the one warm element
   already painted on the page — the warmth must be found in the drawing, not
   introduced by the engine.
3. **Softness.** The source is a 1180×572 screen recording and the end frame is
   a ≈2.5× upscale. Granular pigment forgives this better than most subjects,
   but it is another argument for a clean export.

**Consequence to settle after approval: the brush is gone.** Gen 05 no longer
delivers *"loaded brush leaves palette toward paper"*, which was Gen 06's
opening premise. Gen 06 therefore has to be re-conceived — and the recommended
form is not a generation at all but the **`FolioSection` HTML state itself**,
which already exists in the codebase as a live layered composition and is what
the film must hand over to.

## Push target (localised)

The **pink tamarix bush at the right of the painted scene**, roughly 65–85%

not the blue-green river band.

## Prompt (paste as-is)

A single continuous shot, no cuts. The camera begins almost still on a
watercolour page in a travel sketchbook, warm ivory paper showing a painted
river valley, then pushes extremely slowly into the pink flowering tamarix
bush painted at the right of the scene. Paper texture and watercolour
granulation fill the frame and become briefly abstract; the pink deepens and
warms until the painted granulation becomes real ground pigment, a settled
field of granular deep red madder powder in a wide metal dye bath, natural
daylight, fine dust and grain visible on its surface. The drawing never
animates: no paint spreading, no blossoms moving, no ink appearing.
Photographic realism at the end. Slow human handheld breathing, shallow focus
in the abstract middle.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Movement continuity** — one continuous push out of the page; no cut, no
   restart.
2. **No visible AI morphing** — the passage rides painted granulation → real
   pigment granulation, not object warping.
3. **The drawing never animates** — the failure mode of Gen 01, which merely
   sharpened its own wash. Watch frames 2–5 specifically.
4. **The pigment reads as real ground colour** — powder with grain and dust,
   not a flat digital red.
