## Prompt (paste as-is)

A single continuous shot, no cuts. The camera begins almost still on a
travel sketchbook page filling the frame — warm ivory paper and a
transparent watercolour of a window view onto a valley — then pushes
extremely slowly into the dark ochre-brown painted mountain ridge in the
upper left of the page, above the painted window frame. Paper texture and
watercolour granulation fill the frame and become briefly abstract; the
granulation becomes real mineral rock grain, painted shadows become real
geological shadows. The abstraction resolves into a real photographic
Ladakh mountain landscape: layered dusty-mauve and ochre ridges with
diagonal shadow lines under natural daylight, the camera still drifting
gently forward toward one diagonal rock striation. The drawing never
animates: no ink appearing, no paint spreading. Photographic realism at the
end. Slow human handheld breathing, shallow focus in the abstract middle,
deep focus on the landscape.

## Negative prompt (if the UI has a field; otherwise appended)

no crossfade, no dissolve, no morphing faces, no liquid CGI, no particles,
no sparkles, no melting, no text, no watermark, no invented buildings, no
oversaturation, no fast zoom, no camera orbit

## Acceptance criteria (the four A/B criteria)

1. Geographic continuity — the reveal is recognisably the Indus valley
   geology of the end frame, not an invented range.
2. No visible AI morphing — the passage rides texture correspondence
   (granulation → rock grain), not object warping.
3. Camera behaviour — one continuous human push; no orbit, no speed jumps.
4. The paper/pigment → rock passage is beautiful.
